# Adding a project to jacquelinesurdell.com

This is everything you need to put a new exhibition or work on the site. No software to
install, nothing to download. It all happens in a browser.

## One-time setup

You will get an email invitation to Pages CMS, which is the editor for your site. Accept it,
then bookmark this address:

https://app.pagescms.org

Sign in there any time you want to change the site. If you ever lose the invitation, ask Ben
to send it again.

## Adding a new project

1. Sign in at app.pagescms.org and open your site.
2. Click Projects in the sidebar. You will see everything currently on the site.
3. Click the Add button.
4. Fill in the fields:

   Title. The name as you want it to read on the page.

   Year. Usually just the year, for example 2026.

   Category. Exhibitions, Installations, or Select Works. This decides which section of the
   navigation it appears under.

   Venue. Only for exhibitions, for example SECRIST | BEACH, Chicago, IL. Leave it blank
   otherwise.

   Synopsis. The short paragraph that appears above the images. Exhibitions usually have one.
   Individual works usually leave it blank.

   Images. Click the field, then upload. You can select many at once. After they upload, drag
   them into the order you want. The first image is the one that leads the page and shows in
   the index grid.

   Medium and Dimensions. For individual works, for example cotton cord, nylon cord, steel and
   43 x 35 x 12 inches. Leave blank for exhibitions.

   Show in the index. Leave this on. Turn it off only if you want a project to exist on the
   site but stay out of the navigation list.

   Position. Leave this blank. A new project automatically goes to the top of the list.

5. Click Save. Your changes are stored, but the public site has not changed yet.

## Publishing

Nothing you do is visible on jacquelinesurdell.com until you publish.

When you are happy with the project, click Publish site. Confirm, and the site rebuilds. It
usually takes a few minutes. Refresh jacquelinesurdell.com and your project is live.

You can save as many times as you like before publishing. Save often, publish when ready.

## A few useful things

Photo size does not matter. Upload the full-size files straight off your camera or phone. They
are resized automatically when you publish, so the site stays fast. Your originals are kept.

To change an existing project, open Projects, click its name, edit, save, publish.

To take a project down, open it and turn off Show in the index. That removes it from the
navigation. Ask Ben if you want it deleted entirely.

Reordering projects is done with the Position field, but you should rarely need it. If the
order looks wrong, ask Ben rather than guessing.

If something looks broken after publishing, it can be undone. Nothing you do in the editor is
permanent, so do not worry about experimenting.

## Who to ask

Anything you are unsure about, ask Ben before publishing. It is much easier to fix something
before it is public than after.
